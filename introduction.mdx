ChangeGuard AI evaluates a proposed Kubernetes change against what is actually happening in the cluster it will land in, and tells you what is risky and why — before it executes. It also records every deployment, config and GitOps change across your fleet, links each failing workload back to the change that caused it, and explains the root cause with cited evidence.

<Note>
  **Who these docs are for.** Platform/Kubernetes operators (install & operate) and developers / on-call engineers (day-to-day use). If you're evaluating ChangeGuard AI for your organization, start at [changeguard.ai](https://changeguard.ai).
</Note>

## How it works, in one line

A lightweight, read-only **collector** runs in your cluster (installed by the ChangeGuard AI **operator**) and pushes cluster state and your changes over HTTPS to the ChangeGuard AI control plane, which does the correlation, scoring, and analysis. Your source code, secret values, and workload data stay in your cluster. See [Architecture & data flow](/get-started/architecture).

## Time to value

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install — one command">
    A single `curl` or `helm` command installs the operator and the read-only collector. See [Install ChangeGuard AI](/get-started/install).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cluster connects — seconds to ~2 minutes">
    The collector starts and pushes its first snapshot. On a typical cluster this is well under a minute; a first-ever install can take up to ~2 minutes while container images pull.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First readiness score — within ~10 seconds of connecting">
    Your cluster appears in **Fleet** with a deterministic readiness score, computed from that first snapshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First change and incident correlation">
    As deploys, config, and GitOps changes flow in, ChangeGuard AI links each failing workload to the change that caused it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  ChangeGuard AI is **advisory by default** (autonomy level _Advise_). It changes none of your workloads until you explicitly opt into remediation and grant the RBAC.
</Tip>
