ChangeGuard AI evaluates a proposed Kubernetes change against what is actually happening in the cluster it will land in, and tells you what is risky and why — before it executes. It also records every deployment, config and GitOps change across your fleet, links each failing workload back to the change that caused it, and explains the root cause with cited evidence.

<Note>
  **Who these docs are for.** Platform/Kubernetes operators (install & operate) and developers / on-call engineers (day-to-day use). If you're evaluating ChangeGuard AI for your organization, start at [changeguard.ai](https://changeguard.ai).
</Note>

## How it works, in one line

**ChangeGuard Fleet** connects centrally to your Kubernetes API using customer-authorized, read-only access — for EKS, with short-lived AWS and Kubernetes credentials — and collects the environment evidence needed to judge proposed changes. **No ChangeGuard agent is required in the cluster for standard Kubernetes API-state evidence.** Your source code, Secret values, and workload data stay in your cluster. See [Connect your EKS fleet](/get-started/connect-your-fleet).

**ChangeGuard Edge** is optional, for evidence that requires local presence — runtime signals (Falco) and node-level benchmarks (kube-bench) — or for environments the Fleet service cannot reach centrally. See [ChangeGuard Edge](/get-started/install).

## Time to value

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect — your AWS account, once">
    Create one IAM role from the trust policy ChangeGuard AI shows you. It grants discovery only — listing and describing your EKS clusters — and only ChangeGuard AI's principal with your unique external ID can assume it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover and select">
    ChangeGuard AI discovers your EKS fleet centrally. You choose which clusters it may observe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize — a generated script you run">
    ChangeGuard AI generates the exact read-only authorization for your selected clusters. You run it with your own credentials — cluster access is always a grant you make, never one we take.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First evidence and readiness score — about a minute">
    Fleet begins collecting; your clusters appear in the dashboard with a deterministic readiness score, and judgments carry live environment context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  ChangeGuard AI is **advisory by default** (autonomy level _Advise_). It changes none of your workloads until you explicitly opt into remediation and grant the RBAC.
</Tip>
