The **Engineering Advisor** is the judgment layer on top of the pre-flight check: alongside the **SHIP / HOLD / BLOCK** verdict and the CSC Score, it gives the read a senior engineer would — grounded in what's actually live in your cluster right now (active incidents, recent failures, what this change touches), and honest about what it can't see.

An illustrative example of the Advisor's note on a pre-flight check:

> **I'd wait.** `payments-api` has an active incident, and this change touches the same service. Nothing here contradicts the score — but the score can't see the incident timeline. Ship after the incident closes.

## Silence is deliberate

If the Advisor has nothing material to add beyond the score and the verdict, it stays **silent**. Restraint is the feature — you should never see filler advice, and when a note does appear it's because something changed the decision.

## Availability — generally available

The Engineering Advisor is **generally available on the Professional and Enterprise plans** (since 2026-11-07). It is on by default for those plans, and an admin can turn it off at any time under **Settings → Engineering assistance**; every change is recorded in the audit trail with who made it. The evaluation plan shows where the Advisor appears and which plan includes it, and never spends a model call on its behalf.

What you will see, and why:

- **A note** when the Advisor has something material to add: the pre-flight response carries `advice_status: spoke` and every point cites the record it came from.
- **Nothing** when it does not (`advice_status: silent`): restraint is the feature.
- **Nothing, and a reason** when it could not run: `no_environment_evidence` (the check had no live environment to read), or `unavailable` (your plan does not include it, an admin turned it off, or the platform paused it). The product tells you which.

What every plan has, GA:

- The **pre-flight check** — an on-demand SHIP / HOLD / BLOCK verdict with the score and reasons. See [Connect your first cluster](/get-started/connect-first-cluster#step-3--see-your-cluster-in-the-product).
- **Incident investigation** — root cause with **cited evidence**, a proposed fix, and how to verify it. See [Your First Recommendation](/get-started/first-30-minutes).

## Related capabilities

The Advisor is one of a set of judgment capabilities that are generally available on the Professional and Enterprise plans — **Engineering Opinion** (an owned, evidence-tied position on an incident), **Engineering Experience** (institutional memory from similar past incidents) and **Engineering Reasoning** (the confidence factors and "why not the obvious move"). Each has its own switch under **Settings → Engineering assistance**; Reasoning and Experience need Opinion. The full list is in [Core concepts](/concepts/overview).
